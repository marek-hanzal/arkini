# Serakki 0.5.1

<!-- Last reviewed commit: a4ed2d087e8dc11633e4524b90ba352020d3a354 -->

Make room for what you want to build. Every item now takes its own Board cell, and authors can create reusable Boards that change or reset as you play. Your games and Editor projects now live together, with a quick way to play your latest build or send diagnostics when something goes wrong.

## For players

- Choose **Continue** to pick up your current save, or **New Game** to start fresh. Starting over asks for confirmation when you already have saves.
- Find installed games and Editor projects together in **Your games**. Main-menu shortcuts: `C` Continue, `G` Your games, `S` Settings, `A` About, and `E` Exit.
- Keep your supplies on the Board: there is no Inventory or Toolbar to tuck them away in. Use portals to move items between spaces.
- Each item needs its own cell. Producing five logs gives you five separate logs; dropping matching items together no longer stacks them, and stack splitting is gone. Leave room for output and borrowed materials coming back from work.
- Left click a Board item to open Item Detail; right click to use it, including portals. `Ctrl + right click` fills its production queue. Right click inside Item Detail to close it, except on an input slot.
- In Item Detail, left click a material input to fetch supplies; right click it to take one item back without closing Detail. Supplies must be on the same Board as the producer. Dropping an item onto a producer no longer feeds its inputs.
- Move your pointer toward the Board's edges to scroll, including while dragging an item. The held item and drop target keep up as the camera moves.
- You can also pan by left-dragging an empty Board cell. Right-drag panning and left-dragging items still work.
- Zoom limits keep the Board from becoming a tiny speck or an oversized close-up.
- Larger badges make remaining uses easier to read at a glance.
- Newly built games can show sharper artwork when you zoom in.
- On Windows, changing window mode no longer reports a false Electron timeout when the window changes successfully. Your choice is saved even if the native window is slow to respond.
- Need help with a bug? **Export diagnostics** beside the main-menu version saves a ZIP you can send to support. It includes logs, system details, the last played game's build identity, and its save slots if it was an Official game. It leaves out game packages, Editor project contents, and Community gameplay records and saves.

## Gameplay engine

- Production moves materials as individual items, each keeping its own remaining uses and timers. Outputs and returns need one free cell per item on their origin Board; ordinary placement waits rather than spilling into another space or losing part of the result.
- Merge replacements keep the target's cell and identity. An eligible reusable merge source stays in its own cell; ordinary drops without a matching interaction swap the two items.
- Production, merges, depletion, and Clock expiry can mix Item, Space, and Template outcomes: create items, move the player between spaces, or replace a Board. They share weighted alternatives, chance groups, and rules.
- A receiving item can transport dropped items to another space, keeping their remaining uses, timers, and owned work. This does not move the player unless a Space outcome also requests it. Explicit source merges take priority; a failed interaction never falls back to a swap.
- Template outcomes reset their originating Board, even when you're elsewhere. They replace its items and clear its work without refunds or completion rewards, leaving other Boards and global switches alone. A failed reset leaves the old Board intact.

## For game authors

- Starting layouts now place one item per Board cell. Inventory, Toolbar, storage-scope settings, and stack-size controls have been removed from the Editor and project format. Recipe quantities still let you request or produce several individual items.
- Create and edit **Board templates**, each with its own dimensions and item layout. Assign them to starting spaces or apply them during play through Template outcomes. Reusing a template creates independent Boards; the starting space needs an assignment before you can build.
- Configure Item, Space, and Template results in the shared **Outcomes** controls. Portals now use ordinary zero-duration lines with Space outcomes; transporting dropped items is a separate receiving-side Space merge.
- Queries use one distance setting, from nearby cells to `universe` for every Board space. Unit queries can reach across spaces; material Autofill stays on the producer's Board even with `universe`.
- New items default to the **Simple** interface: information without production controls. Their Default line still works through Board clicks; choose **Default** UI when players need the full line controls.
- Hit **Play** on a project in Your games to build, install, and launch it. Builds use the latest saved files, including external edits, without needing an Editor refresh first.
- Builds now keep Item Artwork up to **512 × 512**, up from 256 × 256. Rebuild your Serapack to use the higher resolution; smaller images aren't enlarged.
- Developer settings include a confirmed **Hard reset** that restarts the app from scratch. It permanently deletes Serakki's application data, including saves, managed Editor projects, installed packages, settings, and logs.

## Serapacks, saves, and distribution

- Fixed stable-release packaging being skipped when prerelease checks weren't needed.
- Larger games can now load through the package catalog: Serapack payloads support up to 512 MiB, with a 2 GiB catalog budget.
- The website now points to the latest release downloads and includes support email and Discord links.

## Compatibility and migration

This is still a pre-stable release. Older saves and packages using stacks, Inventory, Toolbar, or the previous output and starting-layout formats no longer match the game format, and there is no automatic migration. Start a new game. For your own projects, move starting layouts into Board templates, assign starting spaces, replace outputs and portal Actions with Outcomes, update Items and queries, and rebuild the Serapack.
