# Serakki 0.5.1

<!-- Last reviewed commit: 7b3bda7bec34d22f9ae41b28f0b1145cf6437cc5 -->

Make room for what you want to build. Inventory, Toolbar, and stacks are gone: every item now takes its own Board cell, making space part of your production planning. Getting around is easier too, with edge scrolling and updated mouse controls.

## For players

- Choose **Continue** to pick up your current save, or **New Game** to start fresh. Starting over asks for confirmation when you already have saves.
- Keep your supplies on the Board: there is no Inventory or Toolbar to tuck them away in. Use portals to move items between spaces.
- Each item needs its own cell. Producing five logs gives you five separate logs; dropping matching items together no longer stacks them, and stack splitting is gone. Leave room for output and borrowed materials coming back from work.
- Left click an ordinary Board item to open Item Detail; right click to use it. `Ctrl + right click` fills its production queue. Right click inside Item Detail to close it.
- Portals still activate with a left click and show Detail with a right click.
- Move your pointer toward the Board's edges to scroll, including while dragging an item. The held item and drop target keep up as the camera moves.
- You can also pan by left-dragging an empty Board cell. Right-drag panning and left-dragging items still work.
- Zoom limits keep the Board from becoming a tiny speck or an oversized close-up.
- Larger badges make remaining uses easier to read at a glance.
- Newly built games can show sharper artwork when you zoom in.
- On Windows, changing window mode no longer reports a false Electron timeout when the window changes successfully. Your choice is saved even if the native window is slow to respond.

## Gameplay engine

- Production moves materials as individual items, each keeping its own remaining uses and timers. Outputs and returns need one free cell per item on their origin Board; ordinary placement waits rather than spilling into another space or losing part of the result.
- Merge replacements keep the target's cell and identity. An eligible reusable merge source stays in its own cell; ordinary drops without a matching interaction swap the two items.

## For game authors

- Starting layouts now place one item per Board cell. Inventory, Toolbar, storage-scope settings, and stack-size controls have been removed from the Editor and project format. Recipe quantities still let you request or produce several individual items.
- Queries use one distance setting, from nearby cells to `universe` for every Board space. Material and Unit queries can reach across spaces when you choose `universe`.
- Builds now keep Item Artwork up to **512 × 512**, up from 256 × 256. Rebuild your Serapack to use the higher resolution; smaller images aren't enlarged.
- Developer settings include a confirmed **Hard reset** that restarts the app from scratch. It permanently deletes Serakki's application data, including saves, managed Editor projects, installed packages, settings, and logs.

## Serapacks, saves, and distribution

- Fixed stable-release packaging being skipped when prerelease checks weren't needed.
- Larger games can now load through the package catalog: Serapack payloads support up to 512 MiB, with a 2 GiB catalog budget.
- The website now points to the latest release downloads and includes support email and Discord links.

## Compatibility and migration

This is still a pre-stable release. Older saves and packages using stacks, Inventory, or Toolbar no longer match the game format, and there is no automatic migration. Start a new game; for your own projects, update the layout, Items, and queries to the Board-only format and rebuild the Serapack.
